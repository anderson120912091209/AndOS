RELAY — talk instead of typing
==============================

1. Double-click Relay.exe (in this folder).

   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" — Relay isn't from the Microsoft
   Store yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only do this once.

2. A small black pill appears at the top of your screen: "Relay is ready".

3. Click into any text box — WhatsApp, Gmail, Word, anywhere — then
   HOLD  Ctrl + Win  (the Windows key), say what you want to write, and LET GO.
   Your words appear where you were typing.

   English, Hindi, or a mix — all fine. Hindi comes out in English letters
   (kal milte hain). Want it in Devanagari (कल मिलते हैं) instead? Right-click
   Relay's icon by the clock (you may need the ^ arrow) → Hindi → In Devanagari.

Good to know
------------
- Everything stays on this PC. Nothing you say is sent anywhere.
- Relay starts with Windows. To stop that, or to quit: right-click its icon
  by the clock.
- More accurate speech: right-click the icon → Speech → More accurate
  (a one-time 574 MB download; a little slower on older laptops).
- Keep this folder where it is (Documents is a good home) — Relay runs from here.

Something wrong?
----------------
Right-click Relay's icon → "Open the log folder", and send relay.log to
whoever gave you Relay. It says what happened — never what you said.
